02Economic outcomes

Where AI changes the economics.

The starting question is not where AI can be used. It is where AI can materially change the economics of the organisation. In our work, the answer comes back to four outcomes.

  1. Grow revenue

    Improve commercial performance through better customer engagement, more effective selling, stronger service propositions and new sources of revenue.

  2. Improve margins

    Reduce cost-to-serve, remove friction from workflows and redesign the economics of how services are delivered.

  3. Increase capacity

    Handle more work, serve more customers and manage greater complexity without a proportionate increase in operating resources.

  4. Build new value

    Create products, services, business models and ventures that AI makes economically viable for the first time.

These are the outcomes our work is designed around. They are not separate services.

04Operating services in practice

From building a tool to running a service.

Most AI projects end when a system is handed over. An operating service begins there: the workflow is run, measured and improved as an ongoing responsibility.

AI engineering engagement

Comet builds the workflow. Your team runs it.

  • Delivered: a working system, integrated with your environment, with documentation and handover.
  • Your organisation owns daily operation, staffing and performance.

AI-native operating service

Comet runs the workflow to an agreed standard.

  • Delivered: the work itself, such as enquiries handled, documents processed or cases routed, reported against agreed measures.
  • Comet owns day-to-day operation, quality review and exception handling. Your organisation keeps the decisions, approvals and accountabilities that cannot be delegated.
  1. IntakeRequests, documents and data arrive through agreed channels.
  2. AI processingClassification, extraction, drafting and routing.
  3. Human reviewSpecialists check, decide or escalate where the rules require it.
  4. Delivery and reportingWork completed and measured against the service definition.

Where we are developing services

  • Customer enquiries, service requests and case routing
  • Lead intake, sales follow-up and CRM administration
  • Document intake, data extraction and administrative workflows
  • Portfolio, public-sector and energy enterprise workflows

06Why Comet

Business judgement, engineering and operations in one firm.

We believe that effective AI transformation requires more than enthusiasm for new technology.

It requires sound economic reasoning, informed leadership, practical engineering and a willingness to challenge how things have always been done.

Business economics before technology.

The relevance of AI is determined by the business problem, not the sophistication of the model.

Executive and operating experience.

Our perspective comes from leading transformation and operating businesses as well as advising them. Recommendations must withstand commercial, organisational and technical constraints.

Applied engineering.

We design, build and integrate AI systems in enterprise environments, from intelligent workflows to AI-native products.

End-to-end workflow thinking.

We look at the whole workflow, including systems, exceptions, controls and people, rather than a single task in isolation.

Oversight and accountability.

We design for explicit permissions, validation and human review, and keep decisions with the people accountable for them.

Independence of judgement.

We evaluate opportunities, technologies and solutions against client objectives rather than promote a predetermined platform.

Research & Development

Building the foundations of AI-native organisations.

Most AI systems still operate as a sequence of isolated interactions. Organisations do not. Work continues over time. Context accumulates. Decisions create consequences.

Current research

Stateful AI orchestration.

Stateful AI orchestration refers to system architectures in which AI operates against a persistent, structured record of the work, including what has happened, what state each process is in and which actions are pending, rather than treating each interaction as an isolated prompt.

Our work examines architectures in which AI operates against persistent state rather than treating each interaction as an isolated prompt.

Current research areas include persistent system state, structured task generation, validation-gated execution, multi-step orchestration, state-dependent decision making and persistent autonomous workflows.

This work has resulted in patent-pending intellectual property developed by Comet Innovations.

Why it matters

Enterprise AI must do more than respond intelligently.

Understand what has already happened.

Know what state a process is in.

Determine what should happen next.

Coordinate activity across systems and people.

Validate outcomes before moving forward.

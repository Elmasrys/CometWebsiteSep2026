Research & Development

Engineering intelligence beyond the conversation.

Many AI applications are designed around individual interactions. A user asks a question. A model produces a response. The interaction ends.

Business operations rarely work this way. Processes unfold over time. Information changes. Tasks remain open. Decisions create dependencies. Actions require validation.

AI systems intended to operate within these environments must do more than generate intelligent responses. They need architectures capable of maintaining context, managing state and coordinating activity across systems and people.

Our research focus

Stateful AI Orchestration

Comet’s applied research examines AI system architectures in which work is represented through persistent, structured state.

Rather than relying exclusively on isolated prompts, these architectures maintain information about the status of processes, completed actions, outstanding tasks and the conditions governing subsequent execution.

Current areas of investigation include:

  • Persistent system and workflow state.
  • Structured task generation and management.
  • Context continuity across interactions.
  • State-dependent decision-making.
  • Validation-gated execution.
  • Multi-step orchestration.
  • Coordination between AI capabilities and external systems.
  • Persistent autonomous workflows.

This work has contributed to patent-pending intellectual property.

Why it matters.

Continuity

Enterprise systems need to understand what has already occurred and what remains outstanding.

Control

AI-generated actions require appropriate boundaries, permissions and validation.

Coordination

Operational work frequently spans multiple applications, roles and processes.

Reliability

Systems must handle exceptions, maintain traceability and behave predictably within defined operating requirements.

Accountability

Organisations need to understand which actions were taken, under which conditions and with what outcomes.

Research with practical relevance.

We pursue applied research to deepen our understanding of how AI can participate in persistent business workflows.

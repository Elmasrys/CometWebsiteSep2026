What we do/Educate

Educate

Developing leadership for the AI economy.

Executive education for leaders navigating technological and strategic change.

Artificial intelligence is changing what executives can know, how quickly they can explore alternatives and the decisions they are expected to make. Understanding the technology is only part of the challenge. Leaders must recognise how AI changes competitive dynamics, question established assumptions, exercise judgement in uncertain conditions and guide organisations through change.

Comet designs and delivers executive education programmes that connect AI with strategy, leadership, business transformation and decision-making. We focus on the questions senior leaders need to answer, not on teaching technology for its own sake.

Beyond AI literacy.

Executives do not need to become machine learning engineers.

They need a sufficiently deep understanding of AI to challenge proposals, recognise opportunities, evaluate risks and make informed decisions. They also need to understand how AI changes their own role as leaders.

Our programmes combine strategic perspectives with practical learning experiences, discussion and application to real organisational challenges.

The objective is not simply greater familiarity with AI. It is better leadership in an environment where intelligence is increasingly accessible, work can be redesigned and established business assumptions may no longer hold.

Our executive education programmes

01

AI for Boards and Executive Committees

Understanding the strategic implications of artificial intelligence.

A focused executive experience examining how AI changes competitive advantage, enterprise economics, governance, risk and investment priorities.

Designed for boards, chief executives and executive committees who need to establish a shared perspective on AI’s strategic significance.

Participants explore the questions that should shape executive oversight, investment decisions and organisational priorities.

02

AI as a Strategic Thinking Partner

Strengthening the quality of executive decisions.

An experiential programme examining how leaders can use AI to explore strategic alternatives, challenge assumptions, test scenarios and develop more informed decisions.

Participants work with real business questions and assess both the capabilities and limitations of AI-supported reasoning.

The emphasis is on executive judgement, not delegating accountability to technology.

03

Leading AI Transformation

From technological possibility to organisational change.

A leadership programme focused on the economic, organisational and managerial implications of AI transformation.

Topics include strategic prioritisation, operating models, workforce implications, governance, adoption and transformation leadership.

Designed for executives responsible for leading change across functions, business units or institutions.

04

AI, Innovation and New Business Models

Recognising the opportunities that established assumptions conceal.

Explore how AI changes the economics of products, services and business formation.

Participants examine opportunities for business model innovation, new value propositions, venture creation and competitive repositioning.

05

Custom Executive Leadership Programmes

Designed around the organisation’s strategic context.

We develop tailored learning experiences for organisations with specific leadership objectives.

Programmes may incorporate industry scenarios, live business challenges, executive discussions, decision simulations and applied exercises.

They can be delivered as standalone workshops, multi-session programmes or components of broader executive leadership initiatives.

How we teach.

{{#images.educate_detail.available}}{{/images.educate_detail.available}}

Executive perspective

Learning begins with the strategic responsibilities of the participants and the decisions they face.

Experiential learning

Participants apply concepts to practical challenges rather than relying solely on presentations and theoretical instruction.

Constructive challenge

We encourage leaders to test assumptions, explore competing perspectives and recognise the limits of available evidence.

Application to real decisions

Where appropriate, learning activities use participants’ actual business challenges, within agreed confidentiality and data-handling boundaries.

Technology in context

We examine AI as a strategic and organisational capability, with attention to both its opportunities and its limitations.

Formats and delivery

Our programmes can be delivered through:

  • Board and executive briefings.
  • Half-day and full-day executive workshops.
  • Leadership team masterclasses.
  • Multi-session executive development programmes.
  • Bespoke leadership academies.
  • Facilitated strategic decision-making labs.

Programmes may be delivered in person, virtually or through a blended format. The structure is defined around the audience, learning objectives, organisational context and desired outcomes.

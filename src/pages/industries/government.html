Government

Building the capabilities of government in the AI economy.

Artificial intelligence creates opportunities to rethink how public institutions make decisions, allocate resources and deliver services. Its potential extends beyond administrative efficiency. AI may change the cost of delivering public services, improve institutional responsiveness, strengthen policy analysis and enable new forms of interaction between governments, citizens and businesses.

Realising these opportunities requires sound institutional judgement, accountable governance and practical execution. Comet works with public-sector leaders to understand the implications of AI and develop the strategies, leadership capabilities and technical systems needed to respond.

Technology must serve public value.

The objectives of government differ from those of a commercial enterprise.

Public institutions must balance service outcomes, fiscal responsibility, fairness, accessibility, accountability and public trust. AI initiatives should be evaluated in that context.

We help public-sector organisations distinguish applications that can improve institutional performance from initiatives driven primarily by technological novelty. Our approach recognises the importance of public accountability, sensitive information, operational continuity and responsible deployment.

Areas of focus

  1. Institutional AI Strategy

    Establish priorities for AI adoption across public bodies, agencies and government programmes, aligned with institutional mandates and measurable outcomes.

  2. Public Service Transformation

    Examine how AI can help redesign service workflows, improve responsiveness, reduce administrative burdens and support more effective service delivery.

  3. Government Operating Models

    Assess the organisational structures, decision rights, governance arrangements and capabilities required to manage AI responsibly.

  4. Policy and Strategic Decision Support

    Explore appropriate applications of AI in strategic analysis, scenario evaluation, evidence synthesis and decision preparation, with human judgement and accountability retained.

  5. Public-Sector Innovation

    Evaluate opportunities for new digital services, institutional capabilities and government-enabled innovation.

  6. Data and AI Governance

    Support the development of strategic approaches to AI accountability, risk management, information protection, oversight and institutional readiness.

Responsible deployment by design.

Government AI programmes may operate under heightened requirements for security, transparency, auditability and control.

These requirements should inform system design from the outset. We assess technical approaches against the client’s legal, institutional and operational requirements, including restrictions on infrastructure and data processing where applicable.

We do not assume that a public institution should adopt a particular platform or hosting model.

Private Equity

AI as a lever for portfolio value creation.

Artificial intelligence is changing the economics of many businesses in which private equity invests.

It can alter the cost of delivering services, reshape customer propositions, change the scalability of operating models and create opportunities for new products and revenue.

For investors, the relevant question is not how many AI initiatives a portfolio company has underway.

It is which changes can create meaningful, defensible investment value.

Comet works with investment teams, operating partners and portfolio company executives to examine those opportunities and take informed action.

An investment perspective on AI.

AI opportunities need to be assessed through the economics of the underlying business.

A productivity initiative may improve margins. A new product capability may support revenue growth. A redesigned operating model may allow the business to scale without proportional increases in cost.

Other applications may offer little material advantage once implementation cost, operational risk and competitive response are considered.

We help investors and management teams make these distinctions.

Our perspective connects AI opportunities with the value creation agenda, management capability and the investment horizon.

Portfolio AI Transformation

A consistent method. Business-specific decisions.

For investors managing multiple portfolio companies, Comet can structure a programme that examines AI opportunities across a defined group of businesses.

The approach establishes comparable assessment criteria while respecting differences in sector, economics, operating maturity and management priorities.

Potential outputs include company-level opportunity assessments, portfolio prioritisation, investment cases and recommendations for implementation.

Subsequent advisory, education or engineering engagements can be commissioned independently according to each company’s requirements.

The measure is investment relevance.

We examine opportunities through their potential contribution to revenue growth, EBITDA, cash generation, operating leverage, competitive position and enterprise value.

Value estimates are assessed against implementation requirements, execution risk and evidence.

AI activity is not a substitute for investment performance.

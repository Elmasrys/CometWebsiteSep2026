About {{site.name}}

{{site.name}} is an {{site.category}} firm based in London.

We work with leadership teams facing a change in how their businesses create value, organise work, operate and grow.

Our role is to understand where AI materially changes the economics, determine what that means for the business and help turn those decisions into action.

Our perspective

Business first. Technology in context.

AI is moving quickly. That makes disciplined business thinking more important, not less.

We start with four questions:

  1. Where is value changing?
  2. How should work change?
  3. What does the organisation need to do differently?
  4. What new growth becomes possible?

Technology follows the business question.

What makes Comet different

Strategy through engineering

Some questions require strategic judgement. Others require something to be built. We work across both.

Senior led

The people who scope the work remain involved in doing it. Clients work directly with experienced operators, strategists and specialists.

Operating experience

Our perspective comes from working inside organisations as well as advising them. Strategies eventually meet customers, budgets, organisational politics, technology constraints and operating reality. We design with that reality in mind.

Venture building

Transforming an existing organisation and building something new require different disciplines. We work across both.

Technical depth

Our client work is informed by applied research into AI systems and patent-pending intellectual property developed by {{site.name}}.

How we deliver

A focused senior team.

Comet operates as a focused senior team supported by specialist engineering and delivery capability. Where scale or specialist expertise is required, we work with vetted partners while retaining responsibility for strategic direction and quality.

London perspective. International operating experience.

We work across the UK, Middle East and Africa.

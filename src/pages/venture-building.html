Growth

What becomes possible when the economics of building change?

AI is changing the resources required to develop products, deliver services and establish new businesses.

Capabilities that once required large teams or substantial technical investment may become more accessible.

Established organisations can use these changes to expand beyond their current business models and explore opportunities that were previously difficult to pursue.

The strategic challenge is identifying which opportunities are genuinely attractive and how to validate them before committing significant capital.

Growth beyond the existing business.

Incremental expansion remains important, but technological change can also create entirely new sources of revenue.

AI may enable a company to transform internal capabilities into commercial products, develop intelligent services around existing customer relationships or enter adjacent markets with a different operating model.

These opportunities need to be assessed against demand, competitive dynamics, unit economics and the organisation’s ability to execute.

The advantage of established organisations.

Existing businesses often possess assets that new ventures struggle to acquire.

Customers. Industry relationships. Domain expertise. Distribution. Operational data. Brand credibility. Access to infrastructure.

AI can create new ways to combine and commercialise these assets.

But possessing an advantage is not the same as having a viable venture.

Successful venture development still requires opportunity selection, testing, commercial discipline and execution.

Areas of examination.

Opportunity discovery
Identifying emerging markets, unmet needs and potential new propositions.
Business model innovation
Developing new approaches to pricing, delivery and monetisation.
Venture validation
Testing commercial assumptions, demand and feasibility.
Corporate venture building
Designing and developing new businesses within or alongside established organisations.
AI-native products
Building solutions in which intelligent capabilities are fundamental to the proposition.
Commercialisation
Preparing validated concepts for market entry, partnerships and growth.

The defining question.

What could this organisation build today that would not have been economically viable before AI?

How do you grow?

Comet helps established organisations identify, validate, build and launch new businesses.

The opportunity

Improving the existing business creates value. Sometimes the larger opportunity lies outside it. AI is changing the cost, speed and feasibility of creating new products, services and businesses.

Corporate venture building is the disciplined process of identifying, validating, building and launching new businesses using the assets and advantages of an established organisation.

Four disciplines that are often confused

Corporate innovation
The capabilities an established organisation uses to generate and explore ideas, such as labs, idea challenges and accelerators. Useful for ideas, but not on its own a way to build businesses.
Venture validation
Testing whether a venture should exist before significant investment: customer demand, willingness to pay, unit economics and whether the parent organisation genuinely provides an advantage.
Venture building
Creating the business itself, from proposition and operating model to technology, team and launch, with funding released as evidence accumulates.
Venture acceleration
Sharpening positioning, commercialisation and the path to scale for a venture that already exists.

AI Transformation

Understand, redesign and implement AI-enabled business change.

Most organisations do not lack ideas for using AI. What they lack is a clear view of which changes will materially affect revenue, margins, capacity and growth, and the organisational and technical capability to act on them.

Comet’s AI Transformation work is project-led. Each engagement is built around a defined question or programme of change, with agreed scope, deliverables and decision points. Any of the five capabilities below can be commissioned on its own.

The problems we address.

The most important AI decisions are rarely confined to technology.

Investment without an economic case.
Spending on AI grows while the link to revenue, cost-to-serve or enterprise value remains unclear.
Pilots that never reach operation.
Prototypes demonstrate what is technically possible but are not integrated into the systems, controls and workflows where value is created.
Operating models built for an earlier cost structure.
Roles, processes and decision rights still assume that analysis, drafting and coordination are scarce and expensive.
Leaders asked to decide without a shared understanding.
Boards and executive teams must judge proposals, risks and investments in a field that changes faster than their information.
New opportunities judged by old assumptions.
Products, services and ventures that were previously uneconomic are dismissed before anyone has tested the new economics.

Five capabilities, each commissioned on its own terms.

The capabilities are not stages. An engagement may involve one of them or several, depending on the requirement.

AI Strategy & Economics

Decisions grounded in business economics.

We help boards, investors and executive teams understand how AI changes their business economics, competitive position and investment priorities, and make the decisions that follow. Our advice is independent of any particular AI model, software vendor or implementation platform.

Typical work

  • Enterprise AI strategy and investment priorities
  • AI opportunity and investment evaluation
  • Business model implications and value creation
  • Portfolio-wide strategic assessments
  • AI governance and strategic oversight

What you receive

Analysis, options and recommendations decision-makers can act on: a strategic assessment, board paper, investment case or prioritised transformation agenda, with the evidence behind it.

Operating Model Redesign

Redesigning how work is done, not only how fast.

When analysis, drafting and coordination become cheaper, the way work is organised should change with them. We help organisations redesign workflows, structures and decision rights around a different combination of human expertise and intelligent systems. This is broader than process automation: it concerns who does the work, who decides and how the organisation is governed.

Typical work

  • End-to-end workflow redesign
  • Organisational structures and accountabilities
  • Decision rights and operating governance
  • Collaboration between people and AI systems
  • Shared-services transformation
  • Operating capacity, resource allocation and service delivery economics

What you receive

A target operating model and the case for it: redesigned workflows, roles and governance, the economics of the change and a realistic plan to implement it.

Economics and operating realities first.

We start from the business case and work towards the technology, not the reverse.

A clear economic baseline

We establish how revenue, cost-to-serve, capacity and margins work today, so that the effect of a change can be judged against something real.

The whole operating environment

Recommendations take account of systems, data, controls, people, regulation and the realities of implementation, not only the opportunity.

Evidence and decision points

Engagements are structured around the evidence needed for a decision, with explicit points at which to continue, change course or stop.

Technology chosen for the requirement

We select models, architectures and tools on functional requirements, technical feasibility, data constraints and total cost of ownership.

How to engage us.

Engagements are shaped around the decision or problem the client needs to address.

A board may need a strategic assessment; a leadership team, an executive programme; an operations function, a redesigned workflow or a working system. We establish the question, scope, evidence requirements and deliverables at the outset.

Any capability can be the starting point. A strategy engagement is not required before education or engineering, and no transformation engagement is required before an AI-native operating service.

Value

How does AI change the economics of your business?

Every business depends on its ability to create value that customers recognise and capture a share of that value economically. AI is changing both sides of that equation.

Capabilities once expensive to deliver may become widely accessible. Services previously dependent on substantial human effort may be delivered through different operating models. New forms of personalisation, responsiveness and intelligent service may change customer expectations. These shifts create opportunities, but they also challenge established sources of revenue and competitive advantage.

Rethinking what customers value.

As intelligence becomes more accessible, some traditional differentiators may lose their importance.

Others may become more valuable, including proprietary knowledge, trusted relationships, physical distribution, institutional access and the ability to deliver complete outcomes. The strategic task is to understand which sources of value will endure and which require reinvention.

Changing the economic model.

AI can change how revenue is generated, how services are priced and how costs behave as the business grows.

A service traditionally priced according to professional time may become suitable for a different commercial model. A business constrained by delivery capacity may discover new operating leverage. An established product may be repositioned around a more valuable customer outcome.

Each possibility requires commercial analysis rather than an assumption that technological efficiency automatically creates value.

Areas of examination.

  1. Customer propositions

    What customers need, value and will pay for.

  2. Revenue and pricing

    How AI changes commercial models and monetisation.

  3. Cost and margin

    Where new delivery economics create meaningful operating advantage.

  4. Competitive position

    Which existing advantages strengthen or weaken as AI capabilities spread.

  5. Business model innovation

    Where the organisation may need a fundamentally different model.

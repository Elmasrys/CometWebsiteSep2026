How AI changes the economics of value

How can AI create new revenue?
New revenue tends to come from three places: propositions that were previously too expensive to deliver, expertise that can now be embedded in a product, and services that can be personalised at a scale that was not economic before. The test is whether a customer will pay for the new capability, not whether the technology works.
How does AI change business models?
When intelligence becomes cheap and widely available, the basis of competition moves. A service can become a product, a product can become a continuously delivered service, and pricing can shift from effort towards outcomes. Business model innovation starts by asking what the customer should actually be buying.
How can AI improve margins?
Margins move when AI changes the cost of delivering the whole proposition, not only the cost of individual tasks. Redesigning a workflow so that AI carries most of the coordination, analysis and execution can change the cost structure enough to support a different price point or reach a new customer segment.
How does AI affect customer value?
Capabilities that once differentiated a product can quickly become expected features, while others become newly valuable. Leadership teams need to know which parts of their proposition are being commoditised and where willingness to pay is moving.
Which AI-enabled products or services should a business create?
Those where the organisation holds an advantage competitors cannot easily copy, such as proprietary data, customer relationships, distribution or domain expertise, combined with economics that AI has made newly viable. We test them with customers and prototypes before significant investment.

What we do/Advise

Advise

Independent judgement for an AI-shaped economy.

Artificial intelligence is changing the assumptions on which strategies, investment decisions and operating models have been built. For boards and executive teams, the challenge is separating technological possibility from genuine economic opportunity.

We provide independent, senior-led advisory services that connect the capabilities of AI with business strategy, competitive positioning and organisational performance. Our role is to help leadership teams understand what is changing, determine what matters and make the decisions that follow.

Strategic questions, not technology prescriptions.

The most important AI decisions are rarely confined to technology.

They concern which business models remain competitive, where investment will create value, how organisations should operate and what new opportunities are becoming viable. We bring business operating experience, strategic analysis and technical understanding to those decisions.

Our advice is independent of any particular AI model, software vendor or implementation platform.

Our advisory capabilities

  1. AI strategy and investment priorities

    Define the role AI should play in the organisation’s strategy, determine where it creates material value and establish priorities for investment.

  2. Business model and value creation

    Examine how AI changes propositions, revenue models, cost structures, margins and competitive advantage.

  3. Operating model transformation

    Assess the implications of AI for organisational design, decision rights, processes, governance, workforce capabilities and the allocation of resources.

  4. AI governance and strategic oversight

    Support boards and executive teams in establishing clear accountability, risk oversight, investment governance and responsible adoption principles.

  5. Corporate venture strategy

    Identify, evaluate and validate opportunities to create new products, services and businesses enabled by AI.

  6. Portfolio transformation

    Support investors and multi-business organisations in developing shared strategic priorities while recognising the distinct economics of individual businesses.

How advisory engagements work.

Advisory assignments are shaped around the decision or problem the client needs to address.

They may include a defined strategic study, board-level assessment, executive workshop, investment case, transformation strategy or ongoing advisory relationship. We establish the question, scope, evidence requirements and deliverables at the outset. Our objective is to equip decision-makers with analysis, options and recommendations they can act upon.

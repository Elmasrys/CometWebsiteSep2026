The Fundamentals Diagnostic

Find out where AI changes your economics.

Most organisations have no shortage of AI ideas. The harder problem is deciding which ones matter. The Fundamentals Diagnostic is a focused assessment of where AI materially changes your business across value, work, organisation and growth.

The Fundamentals Diagnostic is a four-to-six-week assessment identifying where AI materially changes a company’s economics and which opportunities should be prioritised. It is a commercial advisory engagement led by Comet’s senior team, not a downloadable maturity test or self-assessment.

The Diagnostic at a glance

What is assessed?
The four fundamentals of the business: how it makes money, how people work, how the organisation runs and how it grows. For each, the Diagnostic determines where AI materially changes the economics, drawing on value creation, work redesign, operating model and venture building expertise.
Who is it for?
Leadership teams and boards that need a common view on where AI matters, including organisations running pilots without a coherent enterprise direction.
How long does it take?
Four to six weeks, depending on organisational complexity, on a fixed scope and a fixed fee.
What does the organisation receive?
A prioritised opportunity map, immediate and longer-term recommendations, and clear decisions on what deserves investment, presented in a leadership readout.
What happens afterwards?
There is no obligation to continue. Some organisations act on the recommendations with their own teams. Others ask Comet to help with the work the Diagnostic identifies as a priority.

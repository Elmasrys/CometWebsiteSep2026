What we do

Different challenges. Different expertise. One firm.

Three independent services for organisations navigating the AI economy.

Comet brings together strategic advisory, executive education and forward-deployed engineering.

Each service addresses a distinct client need and can be commissioned independently.

Some engagements require strategic judgment. Others require leadership development. Others require engineers capable of building working systems in complex operating environments.

We work in the form that best serves the objective.

01

Advise

Better decisions in a changing economic landscape.

Artificial intelligence is challenging established assumptions about competitive advantage, productivity, organisational structure and business growth.

We help boards and leadership teams understand those changes, evaluate strategic options and make decisions grounded in business economics.

Our advisory work spans AI strategy, business model transformation, operating model design, investment prioritisation and corporate venture building.

Engagements range from focused strategic assessments to ongoing executive advisory mandates.

02

Educate

Leadership capability for an AI-shaped future.

AI is changing the information available to executives, the speed at which options can be explored and the nature of decisions leaders must make.

Our executive education programmes help leadership teams develop the understanding and judgment required to navigate these changes.

We combine strategic perspectives, practical applications, facilitated discussion and real business challenges.

Programmes are designed for boards, executive committees, senior management teams and leadership academies.

03

Engineer

Forward-deployed engineering for real operational challenges.

AI produces value when it becomes part of how a business works.

Our engineering service brings technical expertise directly into the client’s operational environment to design, build, integrate and deploy AI capabilities.

We focus on business workflows, enterprise systems, AI agents and AI-enabled products.

Engagements are defined around technical requirements, delivery milestones and operational readiness, with specialist resources assembled according to the problem.

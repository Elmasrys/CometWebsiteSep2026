The Four Fundamentals

A business framework for an AI economy.

The Four Fundamentals is Comet’s framework for examining where AI changes four core dimensions of a business: value, work, organisation and growth.

Technology changes quickly. The fundamental questions of business do not. Every organisation still has to create value, organise work, operate effectively and grow. AI is altering the economics of each.

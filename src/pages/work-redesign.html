From task automation to workforce transformation

Five different kinds of change, often discussed as if they were one.

Task automation
Software performs an individual step faster or more cheaply. The process around it stays the same, and so does most of its cost.
Workflow redesign
The whole sequence of work is rebuilt around what people, automation and AI each do best. The unit of analysis moves from the task to the outcome the workflow exists to produce.
AI agents and agentic AI
Agentic AI describes AI systems that pursue a goal across several steps: gathering information, deciding what to do next, using other software and checking the result, while people set direction and handle exceptions. AI agents matter when they take responsibility for a meaningful part of a workflow rather than assisting with a single step.
Workforce transformation
Roles, responsibilities, skills and measures of performance are redefined as work changes, so that people spend their attention where human judgement creates the greatest value.
Capability building
Leaders and teams learn to understand, govern and use AI in the context of how their organisation actually works. Capability is what keeps the change moving after an engagement ends.

What we do/Engineer

Engineer

AI engineering, deployed where the work happens.

Forward-deployed engineering for enterprise AI systems, intelligent workflows and new digital products.

The challenge in enterprise AI is rarely the model alone. Real value depends on how AI interacts with business processes, operational data, enterprise systems and the people responsible for delivering outcomes.

Comet provides forward-deployed AI engineering, working directly with client teams to design, develop, integrate and operationalise AI solutions. We combine technical engineering with an understanding of the business environment in which the solution must perform.

Engineering close to the problem.

Conventional technology projects often begin with requirements that are passed between business stakeholders, consultants and development teams.

Our forward-deployed approach brings engineering expertise closer to the operational context. We work with the people who understand the workflow, examine the systems and constraints, establish the required outcomes and build iteratively against real requirements.

The objective is not simply to demonstrate what AI can do. It is to deliver systems that work within the organisation’s operating environment.

Our engineering capabilities

  1. Enterprise AI Agents

    Design and develop AI agents that support defined business functions and workflows.

    Applications may involve information retrieval, structured decision support, task execution, workflow coordination and interaction with enterprise systems.

    We design around explicit permissions, operational boundaries, appropriate human oversight and validation requirements.

  2. Intelligent Workflow Engineering

    Redesign and implement business workflows using AI, automation and conventional software engineering.

    The emphasis is on end-to-end workflow performance, including process dependencies, exceptions, system integration and operational controls.

  3. AI Systems Integration

    Connect AI capabilities with existing applications, business data and enterprise processes.

    This may include APIs, internal systems, knowledge repositories, business platforms and controlled data environments.

  4. AI-Native Products

    Design and engineer products in which AI forms part of the core functionality rather than an additional interface.

    We support product architecture, prototyping, application development, system integration and technical validation.

  5. Applied AI Prototyping

    Build working prototypes to test important technical and operational assumptions before committing to larger investments.

    Prototypes are designed around clear hypotheses, acceptance criteria and the evidence needed for an informed decision.

  6. AI Orchestration and Persistent Workflows

    Explore and implement architectures for multi-step AI processes requiring persistent state, controlled execution, coordination and validation.

    Our thinking in this area is informed by Comet’s applied research into stateful AI orchestration.

The forward-deployed engineering model.

Embedded understanding

Engineers work closely with the client’s business and technical teams to understand the operating context, workflow requirements and delivery constraints.

Multidisciplinary execution

Engagements may draw on AI engineers, software engineers, solution architects, data specialists and domain experts according to the requirements.

Iterative development

Solutions are developed and tested in controlled increments, with business and technical stakeholders involved in validating progress.

Enterprise integration

Technical decisions take account of security, access control, interoperability, data requirements, performance and maintainability.

Operational handover

Delivery includes agreed documentation, technical knowledge transfer, testing and arrangements for ongoing support where required.

Built for the operating environment.

Different clients have different technical and regulatory requirements.

Some can deploy systems through managed cloud infrastructure. Others operate within private networks, restricted environments or systems with heightened security requirements. We design solutions according to the agreed operating, security and governance requirements of the client. Our approach is technology-agnostic. We select appropriate models, architectures and tools based on functional requirements, technical feasibility, data constraints and total cost of ownership.

Engagement models

Defined Engineering Projects

A scoped technical engagement with agreed deliverables, milestones and acceptance criteria.

Forward-Deployed Engineering Teams

Dedicated engineering resources working alongside client teams for an agreed duration and scope.

AI Product Development

Engineering support for organisations developing AI-enabled products, services or new ventures.

Continuing Engineering Support

Technical improvement, integration and support arrangements for deployed solutions, subject to agreed service requirements.

Engineering engagements can begin directly from a business or technical requirement. A prior advisory engagement is not necessary.

From prototype to operational capability.

A successful prototype establishes what may be technically possible.

A successful deployment must also address reliability, security, maintainability, system integration and the ability of people to work with it effectively. We make that distinction explicit when defining scope, milestones and delivery expectations.

Applied research informs practical engineering.

Our ongoing work in stateful AI orchestration examines how intelligent systems can maintain context, coordinate tasks and operate across processes that unfold over time.

This research contributes to our technical perspective but does not require clients to adopt a proprietary platform.

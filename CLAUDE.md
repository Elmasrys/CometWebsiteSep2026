# Editorial rules for the Comet Innovations website

- **British English is standard across the website**: judgement, organisation, prioritise, programme, per cent, towards. `npm test` fails on common American spellings in visible copy and llms.txt. Schema.org vocabulary (`Organization`, `#organization`) is exempt.
- Use em dashes (—), never double hyphens. Avoid repetitive rhetorical structures such as "not x, not y, but z".
- Publish approved copy as supplied apart from spelling standardisation; never invent client names, results, legal facts or team members, and never publish placeholders.
- The Four Fundamentals questions are defined once in `src/content/fundamentals.json` (Value: How do you create and capture value? Work: How should work be performed? Organisation: How should the organisation operate? Growth: What becomes possible next?). Reference them (`{{fundamentals.0.question}}`, `{{#fundamentals}}…{{/fundamentals}}`, or the same tokens in Insights) instead of retyping them; `npm test` fails on the superseded formulations.
- Wherever the Four Fundamentals framework is presented, include `{{> framework-notice}}` (or `"frameworkNotice": true` in an Insight's front matter).
- London is the only office.
- **Art direction: architectural editorial.** Calm, monumental, spacious; closer to an architecture publication than a technology site. Palette: #101010, #262626, #44423F, #77736E, #A7A19A, #CAC4BC, #E6E2DC, #F5F4F1, #FFFFFF (tokens at the top of `public/styles.css`). White and off-white carry most surfaces; large dark sections are charcoal (#262626); near-black is reserved for the footer and rare emphasis; greige only for occasional pause, never beige by default. No gradients, glows, shadows, badges, rounded cards or decorative icons; buttons are square; images are framed, never faded into backgrounds.
- **Photography** is graded once by `python3 tools/grade-images.py` from untouched masters in `assets/source/`: natural colour (hue kept, saturation eased slightly), lifted shadows, soft highlights, fine grain. Photographs are always published in colour; never convert them to monochrome. Never grade with CSS filters. No code-built textures, glowing graphics or AI cliché imagery; until commissioned photography arrives, pages without a photograph stay typographic. `npm test` checks the tonal range.
- **Image slots.** Every photograph is a named slot in `src/content/images.json` (file, master, ratio, focal point, placement, brief, credit). Pages reference slots only (`{{#images.slot.available}}…{{{images.slot.picture}}}…{{/images.slot.available}}`), never hard-coded image paths; a slot without graded files renders nothing, so the typographic composition stands. After adding a master, run `python3 tools/grade-images.py` and `node tools/image-inventory.mjs` (writes `docs/image-inventory.md`). Never use generic stock or decorative placeholders. The visual redesign is not final until the commissioned photographs are integrated and reviewed.
- **Commercial architecture.** Comet is an AI Transformation & Operating Services firm with exactly two offerings, each purchasable independently and never a prerequisite for the other: **AI Transformation** (project-led; five capabilities: AI Strategy & Economics, Operating Model Redesign, Executive Education, Venture Development, AI Engineering & Deployment) at `/ai-transformation`, with subpages `/ai-transformation/executive-education` and `/ai-transformation/engineering`; and **AI-Native Operating Services** (recurring; Customer Operations, Revenue Operations, Business Process Operations, Industry Workflows) at `/operating-services`. Never number the capabilities or present them as stages. "Advise", "Educate" and "Engineer" are retired as customer-facing names; `/advise`, `/educate`, `/engineer` and `/what-we-do` redirect permanently (`LEGACY_REDIRECTS` in `app.js`). The four economic outcomes (grow revenue, improve margins, increase capacity, build new value) are not services or packages.
- **Operating Services honesty.** Treat operating services as a developing offer: distinguish what is available now (assessment, workflow design, engineering) from what is in development (recurring operation) and from illustrative examples. Never claim operating contracts, service levels, automation rates, client outcomes or scale without evidence, and never name Anthropic or Hg (strategic context only). Regulated decisions, approvals, statutory decisions and control of critical infrastructure always stay with the client.
- Each main page keeps its own composition (framed photograph on Home, photographic plate on About, slot-driven photography elsewhere, typographic where no photograph exists). Strategic statements use Cormorant Garamond; operational detail (labels, workflow steps, tables, measures) uses Inter.
- No inline `style` attributes (the Content Security Policy blocks them); `npm test` checks this.
